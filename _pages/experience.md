### Generative Image Compression at Ultra-Low Bitrates

*Sep. 2025 – Feb. 2026*

- Developed a dual-representation framework combining explicit semantics with implicit textures to address semantic drift and missing detail. Used latent features and text for semantic transmission, and reverse-channel coding of diffusion-trajectory noise for texture reconstruction.
- Designed a plug-and-play distortion–perception control module. Achieved **29.9% bitrate savings at comparable quality** in the reported evaluation, while preserving semantic consistency and realistic textures.
- Outcome: **Dual-Representation Image Compression at Ultra-Low Bitrates via Explicit Semantics and Implicit Textures** (**TCSVT 2026**, first author).

### Controllable Distortion–Perception Tradeoff for Neural Image Compression

*Jul. 2024 – Dec. 2024*

- Developed a latent-diffusion-based feature transformation and adaptive fusion module at the decoder, enabling continuous distortion–perception control during inference without retraining the original codec.
- Used an auxiliary encoder to provide perception-oriented training guidance, achieving a wide controllable reconstruction range while preserving the original codec’s compression capabilities.
- Outcomes: **Controllable Distortion-Perception Tradeoff Through Latent Diffusion for Neural Image Compression** (**AAAI 2025**, first author) and the granted patent **CN119211548B** (first student inventor).
